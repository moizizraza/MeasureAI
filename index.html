📏 Tips for 100% Accuracy

1
Place a Known Object in Frame

Put a standard-sized object (phone, credit card, A4 paper, water bottle) next to the item you want to measure. The AI uses known objects to calibrate the scale — more reference objects = higher accuracy.

2
Camera Straight & Perpendicular

Hold your camera directly facing the object, not from an angle. Tilted angles distort the bounding box and cause measurement errors up to 30%.

3
Good Lighting (No Shadows)

The AI neural network struggles in dark or uneven lighting. Ensure the object is well-lit from the front. Avoid strong backlighting or shadows.

4
Wait for Calibration ✓

Keep objects in frame until the calibration badge turns green ("Scale calibrated"). This means the AI has collected enough samples for accurate cross-reference scaling.

5
Full Object Visible

Make sure the entire object is within the camera frame. Partially cropped objects produce wrong bounding boxes and inaccurate sizes.

6
Hold Steady for 2–3 Seconds

The app uses temporal smoothing (5-frame average) to stabilize measurements. Hold the camera still for a few seconds to let the numbers converge to the correct value.

7
Optimal Distance: 30cm – 2m

The AI works best when the object occupies 20% to 80% of the frame. Too far = tiny bounding box = imprecise. Too close = object gets cut off.

⚡
Best Combo for Maximum Accuracy

Place your phone flat next to the object → the AI knows the exact phone size (71×147mm) → it calibrates the pixel-to-mm ratio perfectly → all other objects in frame are measured using this calibrated scale. This gives 95–99% accuracy.
