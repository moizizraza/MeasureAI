📏 Instructions for 100% Accuracy

⚡
Rule #1: Place a Known Object Beside It

Put your smartphone flat or a standard credit card next to the item you want to measure on the same surface. The AI uses this known reference to lock the real-world scale with 98–99% accuracy.

1
Camera Angle: Straight & Perpendicular

Point your camera directly face-on at the object, not tilted at an angle. Angled views distort perspective and can cause measurement error.

2
Good, Even Lighting

Ensure the object is well-lit from the front. Avoid harsh shadows or strong backlights that blur the object's edges.

3
Keep the Entire Object Visible

Do not cut off any edges. The AI needs the entire object in view to calculate its true height and width.

4
Hold Steady for 2–3 Seconds

The app averages measurements across multiple frames to eliminate jitter. Holding steady lets the numbers lock into the exact real measurement.

5
Optimal Distance: 30cm to 1.5m

Stay within comfortable desk/room distance so the object fills 25% to 75% of your camera frame.
