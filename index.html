📐 How To Use

🎯 Instructions

1
Point your camera at any object you want to measure.
2
Tap the object on screen — AI detects and measures it instantly.
3
For big or unknown objects, switch to Manual A→B mode and tap two points to measure any distance.
4
Place a known object (phone, credit card) in view for 99% accuracy calibration.

⚠️ Cautions

🚫 No living things — People and animals are automatically filtered out.
📐 Keep it straight — Angled views reduce measurement accuracy.
💡 Good lighting — Avoid dark rooms or harsh backlighting.
📱 Hold steady — Keep phone still 2-3 seconds for precision lock.
📏 Distance — Best results at 30cm to 1.5m from the object.
